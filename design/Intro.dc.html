PROBLEM SET
NUCLEAR SCIENCE · PHYSM0047

Reason first, calculate second.

Two problems from nuclear physics. In each one you say what you think is going on — and why — before any numbers unlock. At the end, you hold your calculation up against your own predictions and work out where they agree.

How each problem works

Five stages. Each one unlocks only when you’ve committed to the one before, and commitments can’t be changed later.

  1. I Frame the physics Commit to a hypothesis and say why.
  2. II Estimate first Orders of magnitude before any arithmetic.
  3. III Formulate Assemble the expressions that govern it.
  4. IV Solve Only now, the numbers.
  5. V Reconcile Check your result against your own predictions.
A coach is on hand at every stage. It reads what you’ve committed and answers only with questions — never the answer — so the thinking stays yours.
